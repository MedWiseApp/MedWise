# MedWise Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 3, 2026

This policy explains how the MedWise mobile app ("MedWise", "the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and shares your information. It applies only to the MedWise app for Android and iOS. By creating an account or using the app you agree to the practices described here.

> **Medical disclaimer.** MedWise provides general health information and AI-generated suggestions. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.

## 1. Information we collect

| Category | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account information | Email address and password (or Google account identifier, name and email if you use Google Sign-In), phone number if you verify by one-time code, and a unique user ID. | Create and secure your account, verify you are a real user, and sync your data. |
| Health profile (optional) | Age, sex, weight, allergies and medical conditions you enter. | Personalise the app and improve AI suggestions. |
| Symptom information | Symptoms, body area, severity and duration you type or dictate, together with your age and sex, plus the AI results and follow-up messages. | Generate symptom suggestions and possible-condition lists, and keep your diagnosis history. |
| Medication data | Medicines you add (name, dose, schedule, notes), reminder times, and a log of doses you mark as taken or snoozed. | Provide reminders, adherence streaks and reports. |
| Medicine scan data | Barcode / data-matrix contents (product code, batch number, expiry date) and your scan history. Camera images are processed on your device and are not uploaded. | Identify medicines and read batch and expiry details. |
| Emergency contacts (optional) | Names, relationship and phone numbers you enter. | Show your contacts inside the app. We do not contact them. |
| Purchase information | Purchase tokens and order details from Google Play or the App Store for AI-quota top-ups. We do not receive your card number. | Verify purchases and credit your account. |
| Usage and quota data | Number of AI queries used and remaining, subscription tier, and basic request logs such as timestamps and rate-limit counters. | Enforce plan limits and prevent abuse. |
| Settings stored on your device | Language, notification and alarm preferences, and the device time zone. | Show the app in your language and fire reminders at the right time. |

## 2. Device permissions

- **Camera**: to scan medicine barcodes. Optional; you can enter codes manually.
- **Microphone / speech recognition**: only when you tap the microphone button to dictate symptoms. Speech is converted to text by your device's speech-recognition service (which may be provided by Google or Apple); MedWise receives only the resulting text.
- **Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen intents, wake lock, vibration, and receive-boot-completed**: to deliver medication reminders on time, show the ringing-alarm screen over the lock screen, and restore reminders after a reboot.
- **Internet**: to sign in, sync data and reach the AI backend.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, or advertising ID.

## 3. How we use information

- To provide the app's features: AI symptom analysis, medication reminders, medicine lookup, the clinical library, history and purchases.
- To authenticate you, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce usage limits. For example, a hashed form of your phone number is kept in a ledger so that deleting and recreating an account does not reset the free allowance.
- To maintain, secure and debug the service.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. MedWise contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

## 4. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that process it on our behalf to run the app:

| Provider | Purpose | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) | Sign-in and cloud storage of your account, profile, medicines, dose log, contacts, scan history and diagnosis history | Account data and the health data listed above |
| Google Sign-In | Optional sign-in with Google | Google account identifier, name, email |
| MedWise backend server | Runs AI symptom analysis, medicine lookup, quota and purchase verification | Symptoms, body area, severity, duration, age, sex, language; medicine product codes (GTIN) and country; purchase tokens; your authentication token |
| AI model provider(s) used by the backend | Generate symptom suggestions, possible conditions and medicine information | The symptom and demographic details above (not your name, email or user ID); for medicines not in our catalogue, the product code and country may be used in a web search |
| Google Play / Apple App Store | Processing and verifying purchases | Purchase tokens and order details |
| Device speech-recognition service | Voice dictation (only when you use it) | Audio you speak, processed by your device's OS provider |

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the public, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of the service.

## 5. Data storage, security and retention

- Your account data is stored in Google Firebase with access rules that restrict each user to reading and writing only their own records. Quota, purchase and diagnosis records are written only by our backend.
- Reminders and preferences are also stored on your device.
- We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When your account is deleted (see Section 6), we delete or de-identify your personal data, except what we must keep for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting reasons, such as purchase records and the hashed quota ledger.
- No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Edit or delete** individual medicines, dose logs and emergency contacts inside the app. You can edit your health profile at any time or leave it blank.
- **Permissions** can be revoked in your device's system settings. Features that depend on them will stop working.
- **Access, correction, export and account deletion:** email [help@medwise.app](mailto:help@medwise.app) from the address linked to your account. We will respond within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your profile, medicines, dose log, contacts, scan history and diagnosis history.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have additional rights, including to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise them.

## 7. Health data and consent

Symptoms, medications, allergies and conditions are health information. We process them only to provide the features you request, based on your consent when you enter the data. You can withdraw consent by deleting the data or your account. MedWise is not a healthcare provider and is not a "covered entity" under HIPAA.

## 8. Children

MedWise is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and we will notify you in the app of material changes.
