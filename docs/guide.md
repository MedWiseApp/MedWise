# MedWise user guide

MedWise helps you keep your health information and related tasks in one place.
This guide covers the common workflows for the MedWise mobile app and web app.

## Getting started

1. Open the MedWise mobile app or the MedWise web app.
2. Create an account or sign in with your existing MedWise account.
3. Complete the requested profile details.
4. Review your information and update anything that is incorrect.

Use the same sign-in details on mobile and the web. Sign out when using a
shared or public device.

## Choosing an experience

### Mobile app

The mobile app is useful when you need quick access while travelling or away
from your computer. Use it to:

- Review your health information.
- Check current tasks and reminders.
- Add or update information soon after an appointment.
- Receive app notifications, when enabled.

Keep the app updated through your device's app store. Allow notifications only
if they are appropriate for your privacy needs.

### Web app

The web app is useful for reviewing information in more detail or completing
longer forms. Use it to:

- Sign in from a supported browser.
- Review and update your information.
- Work through available tasks and forms.
- Check that recent changes have synchronized.

Use a current version of your browser and avoid signing in on a public
computer. Close the browser or sign out when you are finished.

## Common tasks

### Review or update information

1. Sign in to MedWise.
2. Open the relevant profile or health-information section.
3. Select the information you want to review.
4. Make the change and save it.
5. Reopen the section to confirm that the updated value is shown.

If you use both apps, allow a short time for the change to appear on the other
device or browser. Do not enter the same update repeatedly while
synchronization is in progress.

### Complete a task or form

1. Open the task list.
2. Select a task to see its instructions.
3. Enter the requested information.
4. Save your progress when available.
5. Review your answers and submit the task.

Only submit information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge. Contact
your care team if a form asks for information you do not understand.

## Account and privacy

- Use a unique, strong password and keep it private.
- Never share your password or verification codes.
- Review the information before saving or submitting it.
- Use device locks and sign out of shared devices.
- Do not use MedWise for emergency care. Contact local emergency services for
  an urgent or life-threatening situation.

MedWise documentation does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare
professional.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot sign in

Confirm that you are using the account associated with MedWise and that your
internet connection is working. Use the password-recovery option if you have
forgotten your password. If the problem continues, contact the MedWise support
team through the support channel provided by your organization.

### My changes are not visible

Check your connection, refresh the web page or reopen the mobile app, and
confirm that you are signed in to the same account. If the change still does
not appear, wait briefly and try again rather than submitting duplicate data.

### The app or website is not working

Update the mobile app or browser, restart it, and try again. Record the
device/browser, approximate time, and steps that caused the problem before
contacting support. Do not include your password or other unnecessary sensitive
information in a support request.

## Accessibility

Use the operating system accessibility settings on mobile or the browser
accessibility settings on the web. Increase text size, use a screen reader, or
enable keyboard navigation as needed. Report an accessibility barrier through
the support channel provided by your organization.
